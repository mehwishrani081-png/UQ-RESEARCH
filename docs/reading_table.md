## Novelty interpretation

The literature establishes four distinct pieces that must **not** be presented as new individually:

1. Split conformal calibration and exchangeability are established foundations.
2. Image-level inner/outer conformal segmentation sets already exist (Davenport).
3. Morphological dilation as a nested conformal segmentation construction already exists (Mossina & Friedrich).
4. Risk control through CRC/Learn-then-Test and ambiguity-aware CP/e-value methods already exist.

The defensible research gap is therefore narrower: a method must explicitly address the **intersection of spatial segmentation + persistent multiple expert annotations + finite-sample conformal/risk guarantees**, while retaining the individual annotations instead of reducing them to a single consensus target. The exact CoBra mathematical construction must be frozen from the proposal and the cited theory before implementation; no novelty claim should be made beyond what the eventual experiments and theorem establish.

## Why the faithfulness-audit-alone framing is saturated

ValUES already provides a systematic evaluation framework for segmentation uncertainty that explicitly studies data ambiguity, distribution shift, component ablations, downstream tasks and score aggregation, including a four-rater LIDC-IDRI setup. citeturn6view2turn2search2 Christensen et al. subsequently provide a 2026 multi-dataset analysis of uncertainty entanglement using LIDC-IDRI with four annotations per image and show that the behavior of uncertainty measures depends strongly on the interaction between ambiguity and model uncertainty; their LIDC analysis also highlights disagreement related to nodule presence rather than treating all disagreement as a simple boundary phenomenon. citeturn5academia24turn5search0 Therefore, a contribution framed only as “an uncertainty/faithfulness score agrees with expert disagreement on LIDC” would be too close to an already mature empirical evaluation line. The stronger CoBra direction is to move from **audit/association** to **guaranteed prediction/risk control under multi-expert spatial ground truth**.
